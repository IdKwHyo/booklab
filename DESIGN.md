# BookLab editorial redesign

Job: help a visitor understand the research tool, then quickly replay a book and run an experiment.
Reference: Loro Piana's current warm editorial framing, generous image scale, quiet navigation,
serif typography and materials. Do not copy its logo, copy or commerce structure.

One signature moment: a Motion-driven zoom into the alpine image on entering the laboratory.
The welcome screen is one composition rather than the first section of a long scroll.
Root and #top open welcome; direct application links open their requested view.
Main routes inherit a very faint mountain image, ivory palette and editorial typography.
Reduced-motion preferences skip the large zoom. Browser Back, keyboard focus and native
anchors work independently of animation. No animation framework is needed for the research engine.
Terms, privacy, browser-storage controls and a research checklist live in compact native dialogs.
The six construction checks remain in The evidence. Self-hosted fonts avoid external font requests.

Palette: ivory, travertine, cacao, muted forest. Serif display and readable sans text.
Space and tone replace panel boxes. Native controls, tables and actual chart semantics survive.
The alpine image is decorative, not market evidence. Data and mathematical engine unchanged.
